Straight answers

What you need to know.

Do you call prospects?+

No. This service handles written conversations. We text back, answer questions and help people book through the channels we connect. We don’t call customers. Voice follow-up is separate and waitlist-only to protect service quality for our current clients. Join the calling waitlist below.

What happens if someone calls?+

If someone calls the number we set up for your messages, we forward the call to your business line so you can answer. If you don’t pick up, we text them back right after the missed call to keep the conversation going.

We connect and test the call forwarding and missed-call text-back during setup, before you go live.

Which channels do you handle?+

Website chat, texts, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs, WhatsApp and email through supported connections. You don’t have to pick just one. We check your accounts and permissions during setup. Use something else? We’ll check whether it connects before promising it.

Is this a fit for my business?+

People are already asking about your services, and keeping up with them keeps landing on you. You want help answering, following up and getting suitable prospects booked. That’s who this is for.

Are people writing every message?+

No. AI handles the routine messages. We build and manage it around your business, and you check the answers before launch. When a conversation needs your judgment, it comes back to you.

Do you bring in new leads?+

No. We help with people already reaching out to your business. The job is to answer them, follow up and help them take the next step. Ads and lead generation aren’t included.

Do I need a new offer?+

No. If your offer works, keep it. If you want help creating an irresistible offer, that’s included at no extra cost. You approve what goes out.

What does it cost?+

$297 a month. The $500 setup fee is waived. Each paid month includes $50 in AI conversation credit, added after payment verification, plus US text delivery covered separately by the service fee. Extra AI credit is optional, with no automatic refills. Setup and monthly billing start with your first payment.

Do I need to count how many texts I’ll use?+

No. You don’t need to guess a text count before buying. US text delivery is covered. The AI uses a separate $50 monthly credit based on the work it does, so one text doesn’t equal one billing unit. We review the balance and contact you about adding credit. Extra credit is your choice, never an automatic refill. See credit details.

When does the system go live?+

Your first payment starts setup and monthly billing. We research your business, check the details with you, build and test the replies, and coordinate required texting registration. Then we agree a launch date with you. Setup is part of your first paid month; messages don’t start the moment you pay.

How do I cancel?+

Email hello@locallvrg.co before your next renewal. There’s no minimum term. Service continues through the month you’ve paid for; we don’t prorate refunds when you cancel.

Do I need to apply or book a sales call?+

No application. No required sales call. Read the package and start your first month when you’re ready. If you want an answer first, email Adam.
