What you need to know.

Is this right for my business?+

You already receive inquiries and want help answering, following up and getting suitable prospects booked. Advertising and new lead generation aren’t included.

What do you need from me?+

Your business name, website or public business page, and checkout email are enough to begin. We research your business and send you a brief to check. We arrange account access and private registration details separately.

Which channels can you connect?+

Website chat, SMS, Facebook Messenger, Instagram DMs, WhatsApp and email through supported connections. You don’t have to pick just one. We confirm compatibility, permissions and any required registration during setup.

What if it doesn’t know an answer?+

You approve the business information, responses and handoff rules. Custom pricing and questions that need your judgment come back to you.

What does ongoing management include?+

We build and run the written follow-up on your confirmed connections. AI handles the routine messages using the information you approve. We also review the AI credit balance and contact you about optional replenishment. Extra credit is your choice, never an automatic refill.

What does it cost?+

$297 today, then $297 monthly. The $500 setup fee is waived. Each paid month includes $50 in AI conversation credit, added after payment verification; US text delivery is covered separately by the service fee. Extra credit is optional, with no automatic refills. How credit works.

When does it go live?+

Your first payment starts setup and monthly billing. We build and test the system, coordinate required texting registration, and agree a launch date after your approval. Setup is part of your first paid month; payment alone does not activate messaging.

Can I cancel?+

Email hello@locallvrg.co before your next renewal. There’s no minimum term. Service continues through your paid month, with no prorated cancellation refund.

Do you call customers?+

No. This service handles written conversations. Voice follow-up is separate and waitlist-only.

We can forward incoming calls on your messaging number to your business line. If you miss the call, the system texts back. We connect and test forwarding and missed-call text-back before launch.
