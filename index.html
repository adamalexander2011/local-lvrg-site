Who qualifies

We want good businesses with wasted opportunity.

Not bad businesses looking for another marketing trick.

A strong fit usually has

  • Hundreds or thousands of old leads, unsold estimates, no-shows, or past customers
  • A transaction valuable enough that a few recovered customers pay for the campaign
  • Capacity to serve new appointments
  • A generally positive prior relationship with the database
  • A real service worth bringing people back for
  • Proper contact records and a process for honoring opt-outs

This is probably not for you if

  • You have 63 contacts in a spreadsheet
  • You have no capacity to take the appointments
  • Your customers are already furious with you
  • You want a dishonest discount or a promise you can't keep
  • Your average sale is too small to justify the campaign
  • Your database can't legally or ethically be contacted

Straight answers

Questions owners actually ask

Are you generating new leads?

Not in the Test Drive. We start with opportunities you already own. If the reactivation works and it makes sense to feed fresh leads into the same offer and booking system later, that becomes a separate conversation.

Do you create the offer for us?

We lead the offer strategy, but the final offer is created together, because you know your margins, capacity, delivery limits, and pricing. Once you approve the offer, execution is done for you.

Is everything automated?

Parts of the system use automation and AI-assisted conversations. But we don't sell software. We sell a reactivation process designed to move real interested people to the correct next step, with anything unusual handed to a human.

What exactly counts as a "booked appointment"?

A contact from your list who books a time slot on your calendar through our system. That definition is in the agreement in plain English. Show or no-show is your side of the counter; we book them, you serve them. You pay $97 per booked appointment, capped at 10, and nothing else.

Do you guarantee revenue?

No, and nobody honestly can. Database quality, your offer, your close rate, and your market are real variables. What the test drive does instead is remove your risk: if the campaign books nothing, you pay nothing. We also report everything in plain numbers, including what didn't work.

What if my database is terrible?

Then we'd rather tell you than beat it to death. A bad database or a bad customer history will not become valuable because we send more messages. The opportunity review exists to catch this before anyone wastes a campaign, and if we pass on your list, you owe nothing.

How many contacts do I need?

There's no universal number. Generally: more history, higher customer value, and a larger database make a more interesting opportunity. The Test Drive works up to 500 eligible contacts; if your list is bigger, we prove it on a slice first and talk about the rest after.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Local and service businesses where a customer is worth meaningful money, an existing database exists, and there's a real next step to book: home services, contractors, professional services, clinics, and other appointment-based companies.
