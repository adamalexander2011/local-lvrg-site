SMS Messaging Terms

Effective date: August 14, 2026

Program description

Local LVRG and the contractor businesses it serves send conversational and appointment-related text messages: replies to your inquiries, project qualification questions, estimate scheduling, appointment confirmations and reminders, and follow-ups about a request you made. Where you have separately consented, we may also send promotional or reactivation messages about services you previously asked about.

Consent

By submitting a form with your mobile number and checking the consent box, or by texting us first, you agree to receive text messages from the identified business at that number. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Promotional messaging uses its own separate consent where required.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies with your conversation. Message and data rates may apply according to your mobile carrier's plan.

Opting out and getting help

  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out. You will receive one final confirmation message and nothing further.
  • Reply HELP for help, or contact us using the details below.

Privacy

Mobile information collected for messaging is not sold or shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details.

Support

Questions about messaging: contact Local LVRG at the email address listed on our Terms page.
