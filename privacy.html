What we collect

When you use this site, request a demonstration, or apply, we collect the information you provide: name, company, contact details, service area, and answers about how your business handles leads. Our demonstration and booking systems also retain the conversations you have with them.

How we use it

  • To prepare and deliver the demonstration or service you asked for
  • To contact you about your request
  • To schedule and confirm appointments
  • To improve how our booking systems answer real questions

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. Mobile numbers collected for text messaging are not shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Service providers

We use platform providers (such as our CRM, messaging, scheduling, and payment processors) to operate the service. They process data on our behalf under their own security obligations.

Retention and your choices

We keep records as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations. You may request access to or deletion of your information, and you can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. See our SMS Terms.
