Questions owners actually ask

Is this a chatbot?

Much, much more than a chatbot. This is the most powerful sales-oriented conversational AI system on the market, and it has exactly one job: booking you qualified estimate appointments for concrete coating work, 24/7. It responds under your company’s name, follows your rules, and hands anything unusual straight to you.

Does it replace me or my office staff?

For one job, honestly, yes: new lead to booked estimate. It has fully taken that seat from business owners, including ours. It answers in seconds at 2:47 AM, never misses a follow-up, and never calls in sick. What it does not replace is you at the estimate, on the floor, or anywhere judgment and pricing decisions live. You decide where it operates and when a person takes over.

Will it give customers prices?

Only when you provide an approved pricing rule. Otherwise, it explains the next step and books the on-site estimate.

Can it run only after hours?

Yes. It can run only during specified hours, cover missed calls, work every lead, or operate differently by lead source.

Do I need a new website?

Not necessarily. If your existing site can capture leads and connect to the system, we use it. But if you do need website work, we build absolutely top-notch websites for a fraction of what other web developers charge. The even better part: your site is built on platforms you own entirely, and you will never pay a hosting fee. Request a website →

Can you work old leads?

Yes, when the business has appropriate records and permission to contact them. Opt-outs and prior consent must be respected.

Do you also run advertising?

Yes. Advertising is added after the booking process is ready to work the inquiries it generates.

What happens when something unusual is asked?

The conversation is paused and handed to the owner or designated team member with the context attached.

What calendar does it use?

It is configured around your actual availability and existing scheduling process.

How much does it cost?

Two systems, listed under Pricing: the After-Hours Estimate Catcher at $1,497 setup plus $497 a month, and Booked Solid, the complete 24/7 system, at $1,497 setup plus $997 a month with ad spend paid directly by you. The Lead Revival Sprint is priced by your list. Monthly billing never starts before your system passes its live booking test.
